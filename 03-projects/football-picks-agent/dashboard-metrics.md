# Football results follow the published selections

```json
{"week_starts_on": 1, "main_card_size": 10}
```

- Weekly reporting runs Tuesday through Monday, matching the research-card cadence and including Monday night football.
- **Main record:** Track all ten picks on the latest published complete ten-pick card within the reporting week. A later short card or manual lean does not replace it. Each selected row keeps that card's original line, price, and confidence. This follows David's September 7 instruction to track the main ten each week and learn from them.
- On Tuesday rollover, the generated football packet carries the newest prior
  full card until the new Tuesday cohort exists. A new empty reporting window
  must not erase the latest learning record or display a misleading 0-0.
- The September 1–7 main card is the September 5 publication. Its record is 4–6. Sunday extras and the Monday SMU lean are outside that ten-pick cohort.
- The broader audit counts each game date, league, event, market, and selection once at its latest valid line, and remains separate from the main record.
- Review outcomes by market and original confidence. Record lessons after each week, and accumulate evidence across main cards before proposing any model change. A single week's result never changes weights or stakes.
- Only PICK and LEAN recommendation entries count. INVALID and VOID audit entries stay excluded. Historical snapshots remain in the ledger.
- Each publication-date breakdown grades that day's entries separately. These overlap, so do not add them to obtain the weekly record.
- Win rate is wins divided by wins plus losses. Pushes and pending games are excluded from that denominator. Each recommendation counts once regardless of its conditional unit size.
- Recommended menus, targets, and conditional units do not establish that a wager was placed. Actual W-L and returns use only the actual wager ledger. An empty wager ledger means unconfirmed performance.
- Final results come from the settlement feed and source-checked official game reports. Recovered entries identify their original pregame card and recovery date.
